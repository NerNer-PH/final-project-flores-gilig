# [Charcoalia]
<!-- About your app: what does your app do? Example: "Today's carinderia menu and orders, without paper." -->
"Charcoalia" is a simple, responsive web platform designed for charcoal and graphite artists to showcase their artwork and accept customized portrait commissions without relying on complicated e-commerce systems. 

## Problem
<!-- 1–2 sentences in bulleted form. Keep it direct. What everyday problem does your app solve? Who has this problem, and where?
Example: Carinderia owners in Manolo Fortich write orders on paper and lose track during lunch rush. -->
- Relying on social media and personal messaging makes it difficult for potential customers to browse an artist's available drawings. 
- Customers struggle to understand the artist's commission process through informal channels. 
- Submitting custom commission requests with specific preferences is disorganized and inconvenient via direct messaging. 



## Target Users
<!-- List 1–2 types of people who will use your app. Be specific.
Example: Carinderia owners; customers ordering for pickup -->
Customers: customers buys premade charcoal & graphite drawings and commision a drawing.

## Solution
<!-- Add your solution -->
A clean, browser-based responsive website featuring an organized, filterable art gallery and a dedicated commission request form where customers can select their preferred medium, size, upload a reference photo, and add instructions.

## Main Feature
<!-- The ONE thing your app must do. If you removed everything else, what's left?
Example: Customers view today's menu; the owner adds and tracks orders. -->
Customers browse and filter pre-made charcoal and graphite drawings and submit customized portrait commission requests through an online form.

## Pages
<!-- List 3–5 pages and one line on what each page shows. Homepage and App page are required. -->
- Homepage: Introduces the artist's charcoal and graphite art services and provides navigation to the gallery, commission form, and artist profile.
- App page: Combines the core interactive gallery and custom commission request workflow in a single browser-based interface.
- Artwork Gallery page: Displays pre-made drawings with title, price, and individual artwork details, complete with "All," "Charcoal," and "Graphite" filters.
- Fill-up commidion page: Provides a custom portrait request form for entering customer contact info, selecting drawing type and size, uploading a reference photo, and adding instructions.
- About page: Shares background information about the artist, their portrait services, and contact details for inquiries.
